WEMMBU ASSISTANT ASSETS
=======================

1) Logo/avatar
   Replace wemmbu_logo.png with the square face/avatar image used in the title bar,
   sidebar, and chat. Recommended: PNG, square (256x256 or 512x512).
   Exact path: public/assets/wemmbu_logo.png

2) Wemmbu 3D character
   The old skin texture is no longer used. The 3D model and embedded texture are loaded from:
   public/models/high-fidelity_wemmbu_render.glb

3) 3D equipment
   The mace and Totem of Undying are loaded from:
   public/models/mace.glb
   public/models/totem_of_undying.glb

Asset paths are loaded at runtime. Replacing an image or GLB requires a page refresh.
